About

Yellow Tower is Phnom Penh's greenest office address — seven stories wrapped in over 2,400 m² of vertical garden, on the Chroy Changvar peninsula along the Tonle Sap riverfront, just across the river from the Royal Palace.

Work behind a living green wall: natural light through 5-meter floor-to-ceiling glass walls, panoramic river and city views, spacious balconies, and naturally cool air year-round. Backup generator, 24/7 security, ample parking, on-site coffee.

From dedicated 500 m² office floors to private offices and coworking desks — flexible space that grows with your business, at a fraction of Grade A rent.
