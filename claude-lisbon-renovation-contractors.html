Buyer guide: what to check before you pay a deposit

Alvará. Contractors taking on work above minor repairs are expected to hold an IMPIC alvará in a class that covers the contract value. Ask for the number and look it up on the IMPIC public register yourself — self-declared numbers on websites are common and not always checked.

Contract and money. Insist on a contract, quote and progress reports in English, with milestone payments tied to visible work — not a large upfront deposit. A typical full-apartment budget is €40,000–150,000 (about 60–100 m² at roughly €600–1,500 per m²), commonly taking three to six months once permits are settled.

Permits and neighbours. Confirm who files permits and who deals with the condomínio and Câmara Municipal. In pombalino and gaioleiro buildings, opening walls needs structural checks. Reduced 6% VAT can apply to some rehabilitation works — confirm eligibility with your accountant.

Vetting checklist:

  • Check the alvará on the IMPIC register
  • Ask for insurance proof and a certidão de não dívida (no tax/social-security debt)
  • Speak to a recent Lisbon client
  • Confirm the company details on the quote match its registration

Can I manage the renovation from abroad?

Yes, if set up properly: an English-language contract, a named site lead, weekly photo updates, milestone payments and a Portuguese NIF. Avoid paying large sums before work is visible.
